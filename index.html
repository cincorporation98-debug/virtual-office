Frequently Asked Questions

Clear, direct answers regarding legal compliance, USPS rules, and service operations.

Can I use this address to register an LLC or Corporation in the US?
Yes. All our address locations are physical commercial office buildings, not PO Boxes. They fulfill state requirements for business entity formation, Secretary of State annual filings, and legal notices.
Why is USPS Form 1583 required?
USPS Form 1583 is a mandatory federal document required by the United States Postal Service for any Commercial Mail Receiving Agency (CMRA) to legally accept and handle mail on behalf of an individual or business entity. We streamline this process with built-in online notarization.
Can non-US residents or international entrepreneurs sign up?
Yes! We welcome founders from all over the world. International clients can complete the verification online using a valid Passport and secondary address document (such as a utility bill or bank statement).
How does physical mail forwarding work?
You can set automatic forwarding schedules (weekly, bi-weekly, or monthly) or request manual forwarding per item through your digital portal. Mail can be forwarded anywhere in the US or internationally via USPS, FedEx, or DHL.
What is your cancellation and refund policy?
We offer a 30-day full refund policy for new accounts if service has not commenced or if verification is incomplete. Subscriptions can be cancelled anytime with a 30-day advance notification directly through your online customer dashboard.

USPS Form 1583 Compliance Guide

Under US federal regulations, Commercial Mail Receiving Agencies (CMRAs) must maintain an accurate USPS Form 1583 on file for each customer. Acceptable photo IDs include:


  • US State Driver's License or ID Card
  • US Passport or Foreign National Passport
  • US Permanent Resident Card or Military ID
  • Utility Bill, Lease, or Bank Statement (Secondary Non-Photo ID)
